# Decisions log

Format: **what** · *why* · alternatives rejected. The log starts on 21 October 2026, the day the alignment of this dashboard with the other hub dashboards began (house style as in live-greekpower and Flows). Decisions marked *(existing)* were taken earlier, while the dashboard was built; they are written down here so that they are not lost.

| # | Decision | Why / what was rejected |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | This repository follows the **hub house style** (shell, loading overlay, language, accent, modal) while keeping its own analyses: RTE, arbitrage, avoided curtailment, surplus absorption, the CET / local-time alignment. It is handled in its own threads; documentation starts lean (`DECISIONS`, `KNOWN_ISSUES`, `ROADMAP`), with `ARCHITECTURE` / `DATA_DICTIONARY` / `METHODS` later | Same look and behaviour across the hub, one place to read why things are as they are. | D03 | **Loading overlay** paced as in live-greekpower D47 and Flows D03: all five steps visible from the start at half opacity; step 1 *Reading the data* follows the real download of the two files (`meta.json`, `historical.json`) with a 650 ms minimum; steps 2–5 are one per tab, in tab order (Daily Analytics, Monthly Impact, Surplus & Flexibility, Arbitrage P&L): each lights up, waits 20 ms, **draws its tab**, then its bar fills linearly in 350 ms and gets a ✓; *ready* stays 650 ms before a 0.5 s fade. | D10 | *(existing)* **Clock alignment**: market data (MCP, ISP) follow the CET market day, SCADA follows Greek local time (one hour ahead). With the ⏱ checkbox on (default), SCADA hour *h* is matched with MCP hour *h*−1 and the ISP follows the market clock; the checkbox appears in every card it affects and all copies stay in sync. Measured on 1 Jun–30 Sep 2026: ISP surplus hours against hours with MCP ≤ 5 €/MWh correlate best with no shift (0.773, against 0.746 and 0.711 one hour either way); pumping against price 0.505 with the one-hour shift (0.474 without), BESS charging 0.397 (0.363) | A statistical assumption, not IPTO documentation (K07). Its effect on the total June–September P&L is roughly 10%, which is why it is a switch. |
| D11 | *(existing)* **Avoided curtailment** counts only charging in hours where the (aligned) MCP is ≤ 5 €/MWh; charging at higher prices is arbitrage and is not counted. The threshold is one constant (`CURTAILMENT_PRICE_THRESHOLD`) | Curtailment of renewables happens when they saturate the market and the price falls to about zero; charging at 80 €/MWh cannot be avoided curtailment. |
| D12 | *(existing)* **Totals**: a unit's daily charge and discharge are the sums of its hourly values (quarter-hourly in the ISP); the SUM and TOTAL columns of IPTO's files are never added. **TOTAL BESS is the sum of the units** | IPTO's TOTAL row nets the units hour by hour and gives lower values; the physical charge and discharge of the fleet is the sum. Before, the SUM column was counted as an extra charge or discharge, which inflated the daily totals (908 of 1,108 unit-days did not match their hourly values) and gave impossible RTE values. |
| D13 | *(existing)* **ENTSO-E prices**: the API omits points whose price equals the previous one, so missing positions inside a period are filled forward to the end of the period; an hourly price is the mean of its four quarters; a day with a missing hour is not saved (the next run tries again); nothing missing is written as 0 | Before, omitted points were treated as gaps: hours at −0.01 €/MWh became 0 and some hours averaged only the quarters present (e.g. 32.92 instead of 16.46 on 18 Jun). |
